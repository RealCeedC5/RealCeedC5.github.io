Rai Sukjai ไร่สุขใจ

A cozy 2D farming game set in a Thai village, in the spirit of Stardew Valley. You make your own farmer, take over your grandfather's farm and grow crops through the hot, rainy and cool seasons. Fish the canal, dig for Thai gems, cook Thai food, restore the temple shrine, and celebrate Songkran and Loy Krathong with the village.

Open a room and send friends the code: up to 4 players can farm the same land online, with no game server. Every sprite, portrait and note of music is generated in code. The game has no image or audio files.

features

What's in the game

js/net.js

Online co-op

Open a room, send friends the 5-letter code and up to 4 players farm the same land. Money is shared; bags are your own.

js/cutscene.js

Cutscenes

An intro, the mine opening, the shrine, Songkran and Loy Krathong, plus a heart event with choices for each of the 7 villagers.

js/people.js

Character creator

9 hairstyles, 7 tops, pants, skirts or a pha sin, a ngob hat, a pha khao ma scarf, and a cat or a dog. Restyle at the wardrobe mirror and redecorate the house.

js/ease.js

Easy to pick up

Tools switch on their own, hold to keep working, tap the map to walk there. A quest arrow, world map, calendar and difficulty settings.

js/world2.js

Shrine and greenhouse

Fill 7 offering bundles to restore the shrine and open a greenhouse that grows all year. Fruit trees, bee houses and a Saturday night market.

js/i18n.js

Thai and English

Every menu, item and line of dialogue exists in both languages. Switch from the title screen or the menu at any time.

under the hood

How it's built

Read the code →
01

Layered sprites

Characters are painted layer by layer (skin, clothes, hair, hat) into 20×32 frames, then outlined. Any mix of creator options works with every walk frame and portrait expression.

02

A small cutscene interpreter

Each scene is a list of steps such as walk, say, ask, camera and fade, run one at a time. Skipping a scene still applies every step’s result, so the story never breaks.

03

Peer-to-peer co-op

The host’s game is the source of truth. World changes go out as diffs four times a second and player positions ten times a second over WebRTC, so no game server is needed.

04

No asset files, no build step

Every sprite and every note of music is generated in code with Canvas 2D and the Web Audio API. Plain JavaScript files, hosted as-is on GitHub Pages.
