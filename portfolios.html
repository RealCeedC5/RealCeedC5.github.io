Rai Sukjai ไร่สุขใจ

A cozy 2D farming game set in a Thai village. You take over your grandfather's farm, grow crops through the hot, rainy and cool seasons, raise chickens and water buffalo, fish the canal and dig for Thai gems in the mine. Songkran and Loy Krathong come round each year.

Every sprite and every note of music is generated in code. There are no image or audio files in the game.
